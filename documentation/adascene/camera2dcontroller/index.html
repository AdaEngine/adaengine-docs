Camera2DController

Drives a 2D camera: follow, dead zone, smoothing, limits, zoom, shake and pixel snapping.

The controller owns the camera’s XY position, and its Z rotation while roll shake is on, so move the camera through / and not through its Transform. Place the camera at the scene root: the controller writes its world position directly after transform propagation, which keeps the view in step with the followed entities in the same frame. Zoom is the orthographic scale (2 shows half the world area).
