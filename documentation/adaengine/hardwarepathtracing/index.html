Hardware Path Tracing

Let a path-traced view find ray intersections with the GPU’s ray-tracing hardware instead of a shader BVH. The image, lighting and denoisers stay the same; only the intersection step changes, and the shader BVH remains the fallback wherever hardware cannot be used.

Metal, D3D12, Android and the browser keep the shader BVH.
