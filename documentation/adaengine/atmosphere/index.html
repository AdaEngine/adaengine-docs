Atmosphere

Give a 3D camera a physically based sky. The atmosphere scatters the sun’s light the way air does, so the sky turns from blue at noon to orange near the horizon and dark at night, the sun appears as a disc that the air reddens, and distant geometry fades into haze.

The atmosphere is part of a camera’s Environment3D and is off by default. A camera that leaves it off draws and stores nothing for it, and its image is unchanged. The engine still compiles the four small table shaders once at startup and keeps a few small constant buffers, whether or not any camera uses them.

The sky is driven by the scene’s primary directional light, so a scene needs a DirectionalLightComponent as well as the camera setting. A directional light shines along its local +Z axis. Rotating the light about X therefore moves the sun through the sky: with the rotation below the sun sits 10 degrees above the horizon.
