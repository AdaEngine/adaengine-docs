init(_:showsIndicators:contentInsets:respectsSafeArea:extendsUnderNavigationBar:content:)

Creates a scroll view, optionally showing viewport indicators when content overflows. contentInsets overrides automatic safe-area insets inside the scroll content. Set respectsSafeArea to false to omit automatic device safe-area content insets. extendsUnderNavigationBar keeps the viewport behind navigation chrome and reserves its height inside scrolling content. Combine both options to scroll under the bar without counting the host’s device safe area again.
